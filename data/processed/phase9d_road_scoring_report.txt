============================================================================
  PHASE 9D - OVERALL ROAD SCORING ENGINE (explainable prototype)
============================================================================
  Records scored : 8

  1. PURPOSE
     Combine three independent layers - traffic activity (9A),
     weather impact (9B), road condition risk (9C) - into a
     single explainable 0-100 road suitability score.

  2. INPUT LAYERS
     A. traffic_activity : LOW/MODERATE/HEAVY/CONGESTED (9A)
     B. weather_impact   : LOW/MODERATE/HIGH (9B)
     C. road_risk        : LOW/MODERATE/HIGH (9C)
     Each record carries an explicit road_id, observation_time,
     source and data_mode.

  3. TRAFFIC LIMITATION
     Phase 9A labels are DATASET-RELATIVE ACTIVITY labels from
     the available videos. They are NOT actual congestion, travel
     time, road-capacity utilisation, or speed. This report uses
     'traffic_activity' / 'traffic activity signal' terminology.

  4. WEATHER LAYER
     Uses the normalized Phase 9B weather-impact label. No live
     weather is invented; any mock record is kept labelled mock.

  5. ROAD-CONDITION LAYER
     Uses the Phase 9C road_risk (and road_condition context).
     UNKNOWN road data is NEVER treated as GOOD.

  6. SCORE CALCULATION
     score = sum(score_i * w_i) / sum(w_i over KNOWN layers)
     Weights are renormalized over the known layers so they sum
     to 100. UNKNOWN layers contribute no score and no weight.

  7. WEIGHTS (prototype, documented)
     traffic 40.0% | weather 25.0% | road 35.0%

  8. SUB-SCORE MAPPINGS (prototype values, 0-100)
     traffic: {'LOW': 100.0, 'MODERATE': 70.0, 'HEAVY': 40.0, 'CONGESTED': 15.0, 'UNKNOWN': None}
     weather: {'LOW': 100.0, 'MODERATE': 70.0, 'HIGH': 35.0, 'UNKNOWN': None}
     road   : {'LOW': 100.0, 'MODERATE': 65.0, 'HIGH': 25.0, 'UNKNOWN': None}

  9. UNKNOWN HANDLING
     UNKNOWN is never converted to a good score. With >=1 known
     layer the score uses only known layers (weights renormalized,
     confidence reduced). With 0 known layers the score is UNKNOWN
     and suitability is UNKNOWN.

  10. CONFIDENCE CALCULATION
      conf = (known_layers/3) * (0.8^unknown_layers) * min(mode_factor)
      mode_factors live=1.0 manual=0.85 mock=0.55 unknown=0.25;
      floor 0.05. Mock data and UNKNOWN layers lower confidence.

  11. SUITABILITY CATEGORIES (from 0-100)
      80-100 GOOD | 60-79 MODERATE | 40-59 CAUTION |
      20-39 POOR | 0-19 VERY_POOR | UNKNOWN -> UNKNOWN

  12. MOCK/TEST LIMITATIONS
      The scored records are DETERMINISTIC MOCK/TEST integration
      records with explicit fictional road IDs (MOCK-ROAD-*) and
      data_mode='mock', is_mock=True. They NEVER describe a real
      road and must not be presented as real-world evidence.

  13. WHY NO REAL ROAD MAPPING WAS INVENTED
      * Phase 9A records are per (video, interval) and carry no road_id.
      * Phase 9B records are per weather location and carry no road mapping.
      * Phase 9C records are per fictional TEST-RD-* road and carry no video/weather mapping.
      * No shared road_id/location/time key exists across the three layers.
      * Therefore the deliverable scores ONLY explicit deterministic MOCK/TEST
      * integration records (fictional MOCK-ROAD-* IDs), clearly labelled mock.

  14. HOW PHASE 9E WILL USE THIS LAYER
      Phase 9E (route recommendation) can compare candidate routes
      using this layer's per-road outputs: overall_score, confidence,
      suitability_category and reason. No 'best road' is chosen in
      this phase.
