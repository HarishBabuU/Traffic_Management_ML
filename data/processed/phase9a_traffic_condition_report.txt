  STEP 1 - DATA INSPECTED (read-only, Level 8 outputs)
    level8_traffic_over_time.csv  : 15 interval rows; per interval:
        video, interval_seconds (start-end), start/end frame,
        unique_active_identities (DISTINCT tracked vehicles visible
        in that interval), trajectory_observations (raw rows).
    level8_traffic_statistics.csv : overall corrected=122 (conservative=108),
        per-class stable counts, trajectory rows=15491.
    level8_class_by_video.csv     : per-video x class counts.
    level8_summary.txt / validation report : aggregates & integrity notes.

  WHAT THE DATA REPRESENTS
    * UNIQUE IDENTITIES (per track, total 122)  !=  trajectory rows (15491).
    * interval 'unique_active_identities' is ACTIVITY: one vehicle may appear
      in several intervals. Summing the 15 intervals would NOT give 122 and is
      NEVER used as a vehicle count by this engine.

  METRIC SELECTED (fair across differing interval lengths)
    activity_rate = unique_active_identities / interval_duration_seconds
    -> distinct tracked vehicles observed per second of footage.
    Interval bins: low/no-traffic clips = 5 s; traffic.mp4 clip = 3 s.
    Raw per-interval identity counts are NOT comparable across those two bin
    sizes; dividing by the interval duration makes them comparable.

  STEP 2 - EXPLAINABLE RULE (identities per second)
      LOW       : activity_rate < 1.0
      MODERATE  : 1.0 <= activity_rate < 4.0
      HEAVY     : 4.0 <= activity_rate < 8.0
      CONGESTED : activity_rate >= 8.0

  THRESHOLD BASIS (derived from the observed data)
    no-traffic clip intervals : 0.20 - 0.80 /s  ->  LOW (cap 1.0)
    low-traffic clip intervals: 1.20 - 2.22 /s  ->  MODERATE (1.0-4.0)
    dense traffic clip        : 10.33 - 13.33 /s->  CONGESTED (>= 8.0)
    HEAVY (4.0-8.0) is a RESERVED band: no observed interval falls inside
    it because the three clips are strongly bimodal (near-empty vs dense).
    Thresholds are DATASET-RELATIVE; they are NOT presented as real-world
    traffic-engineering congestion standards (no capacity/speed/lane data).

  LIMITATIONS (documented honestly)
    * This is a CLASSIFICATION of observed interval activity into a
      4-level condition label based on DISTINCT VEHICLES PER SECOND.
    * Vehicle count/activity alone does not equal congestion. Real congestion
      depends on road capacity, speed, density, lane count, travel time, etc.
    * No ground-truth congestion rating, speed, or road-capacity data exists in
      this dataset; therefore this is NOT a universal real-world congestion
      detector and is not calibrated to any traffic-engineering LOS standard.
    * 'CONGESTED' here means '> 8 distinct tracked vehicles observed per
      second of the video', which only occurs in the dense clip of this dataset.
    * The HEAVY band is currently unobserved; future data is needed to confirm it.
    * Per-video 'video_overview' rows label mean interval activity; they are
      explicitly NOT per-video vehicle counts (which are 44 / 5 / 73).
